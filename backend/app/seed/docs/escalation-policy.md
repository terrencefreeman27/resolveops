# Escalation Policy

## Always handled by a human

- Refunds, credits, and billing exceptions → **Billing** group.
- Subscription changes and access grants requested of support → explain how the customer's Admin
  can do it; support never makes the change.
- Security concerns (unexpected access, suspected compromise) → **Security** group, priority urgent.
- Anything outside account access, billing explanations, and product troubleshooting.

## Engineering escalation

Product defects and connector failures that persist after documented troubleshooting go to the
matching Engineering group. Include reproduction details, error codes, and steps already tried.

## Priority

- **Urgent** — security concerns, or a full outage for an Enterprise account.
- **High** — a production data or reporting outage for a Business or Enterprise account.
- **Normal** — single-user issues and how-to questions.
- **Low** — feedback and feature requests.

## Missing information

If a request cannot be answered from account records and documentation, ask the customer a
specific clarifying question instead of guessing.
