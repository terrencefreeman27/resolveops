# Billing and Charges

## How Lumen bills

Each workspace has one subscription, billed monthly or annually per seat. A renewal invoice is
issued on the first day of each billing period and charged to the card on file. All invoices are in
**Settings → Billing → Invoices**.

## Upgrades are charged in full

Lumen Ledger does not prorate upgrades. When a workspace upgrades in the middle of a billing period,
the new plan's full monthly price is charged straight away on a separate upgrade invoice; the renewal
date does not change. The unused part of the previous plan is returned as an **account credit on the
next invoice** — it is never refunded to the card.

## Two charges in one month

Two charges in the same month are expected after a mid-period upgrade: the renewal invoice for the
old plan, then the upgrade invoice for the new plan. Compare the charges with the invoice list. If
both match invoices, explain them and point out the credit that will appear on the next invoice. If a
charge does not match any invoice, ask the customer for its date and amount before going further.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Lumen retries the card after 3 and 7 days. The workspace stays usable
for 14 days, then becomes read-only until the balance is paid.
