# Refunds and Credits

## Credits, not card refunds

Lumen Ledger does not refund charges to a card. Unused time after an upgrade, and any approved
billing correction, is applied as account credit on the next invoice.

## Exceptions are decided by Accounts Receivable

Requests for a refund, a credit outside the standard rules, or a chargeback are decided only by the
Accounts Receivable team. Customer Care must not promise a refund or a credit amount.

## How to handle a refund request

Create a ticket for Accounts Receivable with the account, plan and the customer's reason. Tell the
customer that Accounts Receivable will reply within 3 business days.
