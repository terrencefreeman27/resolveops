# Escalation and Support Policy

## Teams

- **Customer Care** answers how-to, access and billing explanation questions.
- **Billing Operations** reviews questions where our own documentation is unclear or contradictory.
- **Accounts Receivable** decides refunds, credits and chargebacks.
- **Platform Engineering** handles integration and API failures.
- **Trust & Security** handles suspected fraud or unauthorized access.

## Priorities

- **Urgent:** suspected fraud or unauthorized access.
- **High:** refunds and chargebacks (money is at stake), API outages on Scale.
- **Normal:** everything else.

## Response targets

Customer Care replies within 1 business day. Accounts Receivable replies within 3 business days.
