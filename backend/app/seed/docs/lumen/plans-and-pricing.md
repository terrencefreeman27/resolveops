# Plans and Pricing

Lumen Ledger is sold per seat. Every person who signs in to a Lumen workspace uses one seat.
Prices below are per seat per month; annual billing is paid up front for twelve months.

## Plans at a glance

| Plan | Price per seat / month | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Essentials | $12 | Sending invoices and payment reminders |
| Growth | $29 | Recurring billing and multiple currencies |
| Scale | $49 | Finance teams that need Insights reporting, the API and approval workflows |

## Features by plan

- **Essentials:** invoicing, automatic payment reminders.
- **Growth:** everything in Essentials, plus recurring billing schedules and multi-currency invoices.
- **Scale:** everything in Growth, plus **Insights reporting** (aging reports, cash-flow reports and
  report exports), the public API, and invoice approval workflows.

Insights reporting is only available on Scale. Growth does not include Insights, so after an upgrade
from Essentials to Growth nobody on the account can open reports, whatever their role.

## Upgrading to get Insights

Only a workspace Owner can change the plan, in **Settings → Subscription**. Moving to Scale turns
Insights on immediately for Owners and Finance Managers. Lumen Customer Care cannot change a plan or
switch features on for a customer.
