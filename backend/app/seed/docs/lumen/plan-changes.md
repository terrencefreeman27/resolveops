# Changing Plans

Revised March 2026. This page is the current policy for plan changes.

## Upgrades

Upgrades take effect immediately. See Billing and Charges for how upgrades are invoiced.

## Downgrades

A downgrade takes effect at the **end of the current billing term**. Until then the workspace keeps
its current plan, features and price. Annual subscriptions keep their plan until the renewal date,
and no partial credit is issued for the remaining months.

<!-- policy plan_change.downgrade_effective: end of the current billing term -->

## Who can change a plan

Only a workspace Owner can upgrade or downgrade, in **Settings → Subscription**. Customer Care can
explain the options but cannot change a subscription on a customer's behalf.
