# Team Roles and Permissions

Each member of a Lumen workspace has one role: Owner, Finance Manager or Clerk.

## What each role can do

| Permission | Owner | Finance Manager | Clerk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Create and send invoices | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Insights reporting (Scale plan only) | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manage members and roles | Yes | No | No |
| Manage subscription and billing | Yes | No | No |

## Roles after a plan change

Changing the plan never changes anyone's role. On Scale, Owners and Finance Managers see Insights
as soon as the upgrade completes; Clerks never see Insights on any plan. On Essentials and Growth no
role includes Insights, because those plans do not include it.

## Changing a member's role

An Owner opens **Team → Members**, selects the member and picks a new role. The change applies on
the member's next page load. Signing out is not needed and does not change permissions.
