# Billing FAQ

Short answers to common billing questions.

## Can we downgrade in the middle of our term?

Yes. A downgrade takes effect immediately, and the price difference for the rest of the term is
added to the workspace as account credit.

## Which currencies can we invoice in?

Growth and Scale workspaces can issue invoices in USD, EUR, GBP, CAD and AUD. Essentials invoices in
the workspace's home currency only.

## Where do we find our invoices?

Owners can download every Lumen invoice as a PDF from **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
