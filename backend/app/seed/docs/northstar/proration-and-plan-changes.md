# Proration and Plan Changes

## Upgrades

Plan upgrades take effect immediately. The account is charged a prorated amount for the remaining
days of the current period at the price difference between the plans, on a separate proration
invoice.

## Seat additions

Added seats are charged for the remaining days of the current period:
`seats added × seat price × remaining days ÷ days in period`.

## Downgrades and seat reductions

Downgrades and seat reductions take effect at the next renewal. No partial credit is issued.

## Who can change a plan

Only an account Admin can change the plan in **Settings → Billing**. Support agents cannot change
subscriptions on a customer's behalf.
