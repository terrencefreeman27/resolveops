# Refund Policy

## Refunds are decided by humans

Refunds, credits, and billing exceptions are decided only by the Northstar Billing team. Support
replies must never promise, approve, or estimate a refund.

## How to handle a refund request

1. Acknowledge the request and confirm the invoice number.
2. Escalate to the **Billing** group with the invoice number and the customer's reason.
3. Tell the customer that the Billing team will respond within 2 business days.

## Annual plans

Annual plans are non-refundable after 30 days from the start of the term, except where required by
law. The Billing team may still review exceptions.
