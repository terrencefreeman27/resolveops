# Support Response Targets

## First-response targets by plan

| Plan | Normal | High | Urgent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 2 business days | 1 business day | 1 business day |
| Pro | 1 business day | 8 business hours | 4 business hours |
| Business | 8 business hours | 4 business hours | 2 hours |
| Enterprise | 4 business hours | 2 hours | 1 hour |

Targets are for a first human response, not for resolution.

## Channels

All plans: email and in-app chat. Enterprise accounts also have their customer success manager.
