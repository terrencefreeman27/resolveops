# Billing and Invoices

## Billing cycles

Accounts are billed monthly or annually in advance. A renewal invoice is issued on the first day of
each billing period. Invoice numbers use the format `INV-YYYY-NNNNN`.

## Invoice types

- **Renewal** — seats × plan price for the new period.
- **Proration** — issued immediately when a plan is upgraded mid-period.
- **Seat add-on** — issued immediately when seats are added mid-period.

A customer can therefore receive more than one invoice in the same month. Each invoice is charged
separately to the payment method on file.

## Charges that look duplicated

When a customer reports being "charged twice", first confirm the invoice numbers and amounts they
see. Do not assume an error: two charges in one month are usually a renewal plus a proration or
seat add-on invoice. Card issuers can also show a temporary pre-authorization that disappears within
7 days. If the customer's amounts do not match any invoice on the account, ask for the date and
amount on their statement before escalating to Billing.

## Where to find invoices

Admins download invoices in **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
