# Plans and Features

Northstar Analytics sells four plans. Prices are per seat per month; annual billing is 15% cheaper.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Price per seat | Seat range | Data connectors | Roles available |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $15 | 1–10 | 2 | Admin, Viewer |
| Pro | $40 | 1–100 | 10 | Admin, Analyst, Viewer |
| Business | $65 | 10–500 | Unlimited | Admin, Analyst, Viewer |
| Enterprise | Custom | 100+ | Unlimited | Admin, Analyst, Viewer |

## Features by plan

- **Dashboards** (view, share, comment): all plans.
- **Reporting** (report builder, scheduled reports, PDF/CSV export): Pro, Business, Enterprise. Starter does not include Reporting.
- **SAML single sign-on (SSO)**: Business and Enterprise.
- **SCIM user provisioning**: Enterprise only.
- **Audit log export**: Business and Enterprise.
- **Dedicated customer success manager**: Enterprise only.

## Plan features versus user permissions

A plan makes a feature *available to the account*. Each user still needs a role that grants the
matching permission. For example, Reporting is available on Pro, but only users with the Admin or
Analyst role can open it. See "Roles and Permissions".
